# Assumptions and open scientific items

- `K9_V1_PROXY` is the current generic-glass approximation (2.5 g/cm3); exact K9 composition and attenuation remain unconfirmed.
- The reported nominal source size is 0.7 mm; its exact finite geometry is not established, so Chunk 1 uses a point source.
- The 100 x 100 mm, 2 mm-spacing FOV endpoint convention and center convention remain explicit configurable policy until frozen by evidence.
- The need for explicit electronics/SiPM geometry is unresolved.
- The adequacy of the simplified photoelectric-only physics for paper-level reproduction remains to be validated.
- Reconstruction parameters and any paper parameters absent from current frozen references remain open.
- The prior full detector/digitizer had runtime evidence, but this new point-source end-to-end production chain requires Chunk 2 validation.
- Reported average sensitivity near 5916 cps/MBq is a future comparison target, not a tuning target.
